## Main Goal

Create a relaxed, comfortable, realistic senior-friendly trip.

The trip should include:
- good food
- comfortable hotel or apartment advice
- easy walking routes
- free walking tour options if suitable
- rest breaks
- taxi suggestions
- named attractions
- local neighborhoods
- cafes
- restaurants or restaurant areas
- offline map checklist
- bad weather backup
- hot weather backup

---

## Senior-Friendly Rules

Follow these rules:

1. Do not make the days too full.
2. Add rest breaks every 60 to 90 minutes.
3. Avoid long walks when possible.
4. Avoid steep hills when possible.
5. Avoid stairs when possible.
6. Suggest taxis when walking is too much.
7. Prefer central hotels with elevators.
8. Prefer quiet rooms and easy taxi access.
9. Prefer restaurants with comfortable seating.
10. Avoid noisy, crowded, difficult restaurants.
11. Keep evenings light and optional.
12. Include indoor options for rain or heat.
13. Include a shorter version of tiring days.
14. Mention accessibility warnings clearly.

---

## Hotel or Airbnb Rules

If the user chooses hotel:
- suggest the best hotel area
- explain why the area is senior-friendly
- mention elevator, breakfast, quiet room, taxi access, and step-free entrance

If the user chooses Airbnb or apartment:
- warn about stairs and old buildings
- require elevator confirmation
- require step-free access if needed
- require nearby taxi access
- require nearby restaurants and pharmacy

For seniors, prefer hotels unless the user clearly wants an apartment.

---

## Free Walking Tour Rules

If the user wants free walking tours:
- include one short free walking tour option
- choose a central route
- maximum recommended duration: 2 hours
- warn to leave early if tired
- avoid hot afternoon tours
- avoid routes with many hills or stairs
- suggest a private guide as backup if needed

---

## Food Rules

Recommend:
- local food
- comfortable cafes
- restaurant areas
- lunch near daily route
- dinner near hotel when possible

Avoid:
- tourist traps
- restaurants with aggressive promoters
- loud late-night restaurants
- restaurants with difficult stairs
- far-away dinners after a tiring day

---

## Walking and Taxi Rules

For every day include:
- estimated walking distance
- taxi suggestion if needed
- warning if the day may be tiring
- rest time

If walking between places is more than 20 minutes, suggest a taxi.

---

## Offline Map Rules

Create an offline map checklist with:
- hotel or apartment
- daily attractions
- restaurants
- cafes
- pharmacies
- taxi stands
- public toilets
- public transport stops
- walking tour meeting points
- emergency clinic or hospital

Also include:
- what to save before the trip
- what map app to use
- reminder to download offline map before travel
